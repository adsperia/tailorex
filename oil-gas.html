Pipe Mills

The Challenge: Maintaining technical precision in high-volume environments.

  • Real-Time Heat Number Traceability

    Identify which heat number is at each stage of the process without stopping the line to check paperwork.

  • Native Process Control

    We eliminate “ghost parts” in the ERP; the system supports your actual forming, welding, and coating stages without simulations.

  • Instant Scrap Management

    Record raw material waste in real-time to prevent inventory distortions and protect your margins.

Oil & Gas Equipment

The Challenge: Mastering the complexity of the Engineer to Order (ETO) model.

  • Quoting “Hell” (CPQ)

    We eliminate reliance on VBA macro-heavy spreadsheets that only one person understands. If that person leaves, your ability to quote doesn’t stop.

  • Logic-Based Configuration

    We implement processes so you stop treating every order as if it were the first, leveraging knowledge from previous projects.

  • Multi-level BOMs Under Control

    Manage equipment with hundreds of nested components, ensuring accurate purchasing of long-lead items without manual errors.
